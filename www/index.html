Everything a league needs

The parts that normally get done by hand, done for you.

📅

Fixtures that generate themselves

Enter your teams and get a full round-robin schedule, or a group stage that feeds into a knockout bracket. Deadlines per matchday included.

📊

Standings that stay correct

Enter a score and the table recalculates — points, goal difference, form. No more arguing about who is actually top.

👤

Every player gets an account

You register each player against their email and set their access code. Their team is bound to that account — nobody can claim someone else's.

📱

A real mobile app

Not a bookmark. Your league runs inside an installable Android and iOS app, with push notifications when fixtures drop.

🏅

Stats that write themselves

Enter a score and every team gets clean sheets, biggest wins, unbeaten runs and home-and-away records. No extra typing.

🔐

Your data stays yours

Each league is isolated at the database level. Admins you appoint see only the competitions you assign them.

How it works

From nothing to a running league in three steps.

Create your league

Register with your email, confirm the code we email you, and name your league. Pick the game you play on.

Add competitions and teams

Set up a division, a cup, or several of each. Add your teams and let the fixtures generate.

Register your players

Add each player's email and team. They are emailed their access code and sign in to see their own fixtures.
